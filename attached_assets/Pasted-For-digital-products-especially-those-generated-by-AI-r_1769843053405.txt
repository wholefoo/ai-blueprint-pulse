For digital products, especially those generated by AI research tools, a standard "30-day money-back guarantee" can be risky because the value is often consumed the moment the file is opened. You need a policy that balances customer trust with protection against "serial downloaders" who might buy, download every guide, and then request a refund.

Here is a robust, professional Refund Policy tailored for your Success Blueprint platform.
Digital Product & Refund Policy
1. The "All Sales Final" Standard

Due to the immediate access and intangible nature of digital "Success Blueprints," all sales are generally final. Unlike physical goods, digital data cannot be "returned" once it has been downloaded or viewed. By completing your purchase, you acknowledge that you are gaining instant access to proprietary research and AI-synthesized intellectual property.
2. The "Quality Signal" Guarantee

We stand by the caliber of our Research Engine. If you believe a purchased Blueprint is technically defective or fundamentally fails to address the specific niche and level selected at checkout, we offer a 48-hour correction window:

    Correction: We will re-run the research query using updated parameters to provide a more accurate guide at no additional cost.

    Credit: If a correction does not solve the issue, we may, at our sole discretion, issue a "Blueprint Credit" for use on a future guide.

3. Exclusions from Refunds

Refunds or credits will not be issued for the following reasons:

    "Change of Mind": Deciding the business model isn't right for you after viewing the roadmap.

    "Already Knew This": As these guides are synthesized from real-time market data, they may reflect common industry trends you are already aware of.

    Financial Performance: We provide the blueprint; we do not provide the execution. Lack of business success or profit is not grounds for a refund.

4. Abuse Prevention

To protect the integrity of the platform, accounts that exhibit a pattern of multiple refund requests or "credit" claims will be flagged. Blueprint Nexus reserves the right to terminate access for users who attempt to "scrape" data via the refund process.
5. How to Initiate a Review

To request a guide correction or credit, please email support@yourdomain.com within 48 hours of purchase with the following:

    Your Order ID.

    A specific description of the data gap or technical error in the guide.

Strategic Recommendation for 2026

In the era of AI, transparency is your best defense against refund requests. I recommend adding a "Sample Preview" feature on your landing page. By showing a blurred or truncated version of a real blueprint before they buy, you significantly reduce "buyer's remorse" because they already know the format and quality they are getting.
